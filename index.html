WearWild Privacy Policy

For the WearWild browser extension, version 1.2.3 · Effective 2 November 2026

The short version. WearWild runs entirely inside your browser. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and it makes no network requests of its own. Nothing you do with it is sent to us or to anyone else. Your settings live in your browser's local extension storage and disappear when you uninstall it.

What WearWild does

WearWild shows a small pixel-art companion, based on a real threatened species, on fashion shopping pages. To do that, a content script runs on the pages you visit and checks, on your device, whether the page looks like a clothing product page. On those pages it reads the product title, price and the printed fibre composition from the page text so the companion can comment, count what goes into your bag and describe the fibres. This reading happens in your browser and the text is not stored beyond the current session, except for the running counts described below.

What is stored, and where

Everything WearWild remembers is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your own device (chrome.storage.local). It is never uploaded. Specifically:

Which companion you chose and the name you gave itTo show the right animal and address it by name

Behaviour settings (rhythm, corner, second-hand search, reduced motion, sleep-on-it hours, mute)To behave the way you asked

You can clear all of this at any time with Reset everything in WearWild's settings, or by removing the extension.

What leaves your browser

Nothing, unless you click a link. WearWild's own pages use a typeface bundled inside the extension, so opening the settings, the popup or the habitat makes no request either. WearWild contains links to the sources behind each animal's facts, to conservation organisations' own pages, and, if you turn on the second-hand search, to a search on eBay or Depop using the name of the item you were looking at. When you click one, your browser opens that site like any other link and that site's own privacy policy applies. WearWild does not open these on its own and does not know whether you followed them.

Permissions, and why each one is there

  • Access to the pages you visit (http://*/*, https://*/*): to tell, on your device, whether a page is a clothing shop and to read the product details shown on it. It does not read, store or send page content from other kinds of sites.
  • Storage: to keep the settings listed above, locally.
  • Alarms and notifications: for the optional "sleep on it" reminder, which pings you later about an item you chose to think over.

Payments and the locked tiers

WearWild is free. Two companion tiers are shown as locked; at the moment they lead to a waitlist and no payment is taken inside the extension. The extension does not process payments and never will move money on your behalf. A WearWild adoption is a virtual one inside the extension, not an adoption with any conservation organisation.

Children

WearWild is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version changes what is stored or where, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. The version number at the top tells you which release it describes.
